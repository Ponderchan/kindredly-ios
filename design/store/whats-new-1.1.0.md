# TestFlight · What to Test (build 10)

Please try, in this order:
1. Fresh install → onboarding → Let her go. Watch the whole crossing; check she lands on Today with three envelopes and that the slot folds away after the third letter (or your first reply).
2. Me → the courier card opens her page. Looks: Sky free, Moss after your first kindred, Mist / Peach / Night should show FIRST CLASS.
3. Me → aura card → Writing Aura: swipe the nine Dimensions; every card with a sticker should quote one of your own sentences. Habits → Scope: sealed pages are OFF by default; turn them on and confirm the counts change.
4. Share card → Save image (allow Photos) → check the card in Photos. Share card → share sheet gets an image plus a line of text.
5. First Class: three fares on the pass; pick Lifetime and the pass should switch to the cofounder's marks. Do not buy in TestFlight unless you mean to test sandbox.
6. Edit profile: the fifty portraits, no black rim on the chosen face.
7. Tab bar: five icons, no labels, Write in the middle.

Known in this build:
- Prices on the pass are the store's placeholders until App Store Connect products load.
- If you had the Mist look before, it resets to Sky unless you have First Class.
- Web still shows the thirty older faces; a portrait chosen on iOS shows as a mosaic there.
- Sticker thresholds are uncalibrated: early stickers are labelled "first signs" on purpose.

# App Review · Notes for the reviewer (build 10 · 1.1.0)

Hi! Kindredly is a slow pen pal app - you write one letter and get a few back from strangers who write kind of like you. Thats it, no swiping.

You don't need a demo account, just tap "Just looking →" on the first screen and everything works without an email. Apple / Google / email code sign in are there too if you prefer.

Whats new in this version: a little animation after onboarding (tap anywhere to skip it), a "Writing Aura" on the Me tab that describes your own writing style (all computed on the phone from your own letters, we dont generate text about the user), a Save image button on the share card (only asks for add-to-photos permission), and the First Class subscription page now shows the prices directly, with restore / terms / privacy on the same screen.

Safety stuff is the same as before: 18+ check at signup, letters are screened before they go out, Report and Block on every letter and thread, and journal pages never leave the device.

Terms https://kindredly.io/terms · Privacy https://kindredly.io/privacy · Support https://kindredly.io/support

Thanks for reviewing, happy to anwser anything.
