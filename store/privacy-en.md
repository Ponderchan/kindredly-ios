# Privacy Policy

**Last updated · September 2026**

This policy explains what Kindredly collects, why, and what you can do about it. ## In short

- **Your journal never leaves your phone.** Not to us, not to anyone.
- **We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising in Kindredly.**
- We collect an email address, the letters you send, and a numeric fingerprint of your writing style.
- Letters pass through an **AI provider** for safety screening and, at a reader's request, translation. That is the only third party that ever sees letter text.
- You can delete everything from **Settings → Delete account**.

---

## 1 · What we collect

**What you give us**
- **Email address** — from Apple, Google, or the address you enter. Sign in with Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one; that is fine.
- **Your pen name, birth date, courier name, and chosen face.** The birth date is used once, to check you meet the minimum age; we store the date you entered.
- **Letters and replies** you send to other members.
- **Reports** you file about other members, including the text you were reporting.

**What the app derives**
- **A writing fingerprint** — eight numbers describing measurable qualities of your writing: average sentence length, pause density, sentence-length variation, rhythm, and how often you use questions, exclamations, and trailing punctuation. It is computed **on your phone** from the letters you have sent. It contains no words and cannot be read back as text. It exists so we can find people who write like you.

**What is stored only on your phone**
- **Journal pages**, and any photos, audio, or video attached to them.
- **Kept moments** you pin from a correspondence.
- Your courier's name, look, and shape; your language choice; drafts.

None of the above is uploaded. A sealed page feeds nothing — not the fingerprint, not matching, not us.

**What is collected automatically**
- **Basic product events** — that a letter was sent, that onboarding finished, and similar. Stored against your account ID so we can see whether the product works. No IP-based profiling, no device fingerprinting, no third-party analytics SDK.
- **Standard server logs** kept by our hosting providers.

**What we never collect**
No contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no browsing history outside the app, no biometrics, no payment card details (Apple handles payment; we never see a card number).

## 2 · Why we process it

| Purpose | What it uses |
|---|---|
| Deliver your letters | letters, account ID |
| Match you with people who write like you | writing fingerprint |
| Keep members safe | letter text (screening), reports, blocks |
| Translate a letter when a reader asks | that letter's text |
| Run your membership | account ID and purchase status from Apple |
| Fix and improve the product | product events, server logs |

Our legal bases (for readers in the EEA/UK) are: **performance of a contract** for delivering letters and running memberships; **legitimate interests** for safety, fraud prevention, and product improvement; and **consent** where we ask for it (photo library and microphone access, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings).

## 3 · Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we have never shared it for advertising.

We use these processors:

| Processor | What it handles | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (database, storage, auth) | account, letters, fingerprints, uploaded voice letters and photos | **United States (us-east-1)** |
| **Moonshot AI (Kimi)** | letter text, at the moment of safety screening or translation | **mainland China** |
| **Apple** | purchases and subscription status | per Apple's policy |
| **RevenueCat** | subscription state tied to your account ID | United States |
| **Cloudflare** | website and API hosting | global edge |

**About the AI.** Letter text is sent to Moonshot AI only for the moment of screening or translation, and only the text of the letter concerned. It is not attached to your name or email. We do not permit it to be used for model training. Wren's other behaviour in the app — how she reads your writing, what she notices about two people, the shape of your courier — is computed on your phone with no model involved.

We also disclose information when the law requires it, or where necessary to protect someone's safety.

**What other members see:** your letters, and — only after five letters each way and only if you both choose — the pen name and face you enclose. Never your email, your birth date, or your fingerprint.

## 4 · International transfers

Your information is stored and processed outside your country, including in the regions listed above. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5 · How long we keep it

- **Your account and letters:** until you delete your account.
- **After deletion:** account data is removed from our live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days. Letters you sent to another person remain in that person's copy of the correspondence, unsigned and no longer linked to an account.
- **Reports:** kept for up to 12 months so that repeat behaviour can be recognised.
- **Product events:** kept for up to 12 months.

## 6 · Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- **See and export** what we hold — ask us.
- **Correct** your account details in the app.
- **Delete everything** — Settings → Delete account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.
- **Withdraw permissions** — photo library and microphone, in iOS Settings.
- **Object or complain** — write to us, and if you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

We do not make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you. Matching orders whose letters you see; it does not decide anything about you.

We honour Global Privacy Control signals on our website.

## 7 · Security

Letters are encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by database row-level security: only the two people in a correspondence can read it, enforced by the database itself rather than by the app. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee against every attack.

## 8 · Children

Kindredly is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

## 9 · Changes

We will update this policy as the product changes, and change the date at the top. For material changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
