# Privacy Policy

**Last updated · October 2026**

This policy explains what Kindredly collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions or requests: **hello@kindredly.io**

## In short

- **Your journal is not sent to us.** It stays on your device (on the website, in your browser), and nothing in it reaches us unless you choose to send it.
- **We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising in Kindredly.**
- We keep your account (an email address, your pen name, birth date, optional pronoun and your courier), what you send, and a numeric fingerprint of your writing style.
- Text passes through an **AI provider** for safety screening and for translation (on the website, foreign-language letters on your desk are translated automatically unless you turn that off in Settings). Our database and hosting providers also store or carry letters; §3 lists them.
- You can delete your account from **Settings → Delete account**; §5 says what that removes and what stays.

## 1 · What we collect

**What you give us**
- **Email address** — from Apple, Google, or the address you enter. Sign in with Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one; that is fine. A guest account has no email address.
- **The name and profile details Apple or Google share** when you sign in with them.
- **Your pen name, birth date, chosen face and, if you give one, your pronoun (she, he or they).** The birth date is how we check you are at least 18; we store the date you entered. The birth date and pronoun also nudge the order of your matches (see §2).
- **Your courier's name, shell, coat and accessories**, kept on your account so she is the same on every device.
- **The first thing you wrote when your courier hatched**, kept as written even if you never sent it; it also feeds your writing fingerprint.
- **Letters, replies and room messages** you send to other members, including photos, voice letters, song links, folded notes and reactions — and, if you trade names, the pen name and face you enclose, which stay on that correspondence.
- **Notes, bug reports, screenshots and pictures you send Wren** — a bug report also carries diagnostics (from the app: app and system version, the device's name and a short log of slow screens; from the website: your browser's user-agent string) — and messages sent through **Write to us**, with any reply address you leave and your app and system version.
- **Reports** you file about other members, including the text you were reporting, and the people you block; and reports other members file about you, each with a copy of what they reported, and whether three of them have stopped your account from sending.

**What the app derives**
- **A writing fingerprint** — eight numbers describing measurable qualities of your writing: average sentence length, pause density, sentence-length variation, rhythm, and how often you use questions, exclamations, and trailing punctuation — stored with a word count. It is computed **on your device** (phone or browser) from what you wrote when your courier hatched, and in the app again later from your letters and the messages you send in correspondences. It contains no words and cannot be read back as text. It exists so we can find people who write like you.
- **Your matches** — once a day we compare fingerprints and store, for each writer, up to 50 writers ranked by how alike you write, nudged by birth date and pronoun (see §2), with a score for each.
- **Which open letters you have opened**, and how many new letters you sent each day.

**What stays on your device**
- **Journal pages**, and any photos, audio, or video attached to them (on the website, in your browser; on iPhone, also in your phone's own backups if you make them).
- **Kept moments** you pin from a correspondence.
- Your courier's shape and the pronoun you gave her; your writing aura; drafts.
- On the website, your answers to the /aura test — until you keep a result and make an account, when they go up with it: the first becomes the first thing you wrote, all of them shape your first fingerprint, and the longest can go out as your first letter.

None of the above is uploaded to us unless you choose to send it: a journal page you let drift out goes to the letter desk as a letter you send yourself, and earlier versions of the app and the website let you drift a line or a page, anonymously, into a shared morning pool, where it stays. Otherwise a sealed page feeds nothing of ours — not the fingerprint, not matching, not us. It feeds only your writing aura on your device, and only if you turn that on.

On the website your browser also keeps your sign-in, language and settings, and one cookie, which tells our site the language you picked.

**What is collected automatically**
- **Basic product events** in the app — that a letter was sent, that onboarding finished, and similar. Stored against your account ID so we can see whether the product works. No IP-based profiling, no device fingerprinting, no third-party analytics SDK in the app.
- **Website visits** — Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to kindredly.io for us. It sets no cookies and shows us totals, not individuals.
- **Notification details** — if you allow notifications, your device's push token and the app language; if you allow Live Activities, for a letter that is out, the lock-screen details we update, including your courier's name; and, whether or not you allow notifications, a record of each notification we queue for you.
- **The language your app is set to**, stamped on your letters and notes to Wren.
- **Standard server logs** kept by our hosting providers, which include IP addresses.

**What we never collect**
No contacts, no location tracking (we read the country a request comes from to suggest a language and to keep checkout closed where it is not open yet, and do not store it; a photo you send from the app can carry the place it was taken, if your camera saved it in the file), no advertising identifiers, no browsing history outside the app, no biometrics, no payment card details (Apple handles payment in the app and Creem on the website; we never see a card number).

## 2 · Why we process it

| Purpose | What it uses |
|---|---|
| Deliver your letters | letters, account ID |
| Match you with people who write like you | writing fingerprint; birth date and pronoun, which nudge the order (writers nearer your age rank a little higher; a she and a he rank a little higher for each other and two people who gave the same one a little lower, with they or no answer in between; on a closeness score that runs from 0 to 1, the two together add at most 0.09 — enough to reorder writers who write about equally like you) |
| Check you are 18 or over | birth date |
| Keep members safe | letter text (screening), reports, blocks |
| Translate a letter | that letter's text (in the app when you tap; on the website automatically for foreign-language letters on your desk unless you turn that off in Settings, and for a kindred's messages when you tap) |
| Tell you a letter came back | push token, app language |
| Run your membership | account ID and purchase status from Apple (via RevenueCat) or Creem |
| Fix and improve the product | product events, website visit totals, server logs |

Our legal bases (for readers in the EEA/UK) are: **performance of a contract** for delivering, matching and translating letters and running memberships; **legitimate interests** for safety, fraud prevention, and product improvement; and **consent** where we ask for it (photo library, microphone and notification access, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings).

## 3 · Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we have never shared it for advertising.

We use these processors and services:

| Who | What it handles | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (database, storage, auth) | account, letters, fingerprints, matches, uploaded voice letters and photos — the apps send these to it directly | **United States (us-east-1)** |
| **Cloudflare** | hosting of kindredly.io and the part of our server the apps call for safety checks, translation, purchases, notifications and the guest desk; the country of each request; Web Analytics visit totals for kindredly.io | global edge |
| **Moonshot AI (Kimi)** | text being safety-checked or translated, with the target language | **mainland China** |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple; App Store purchases; delivery of notifications and lock-screen (Live Activity) updates, which carry your courier's name | per Apple's policy |
| **RevenueCat** | your account ID and App Store purchase status, in the app | United States |
| **Creem** | website purchases, as merchant of record: your email, name, billing address and card, plus your account ID, which we pass so the purchase reaches your account (its notices to us include your email and name, which we discard; we keep only reference numbers, plan, status, price and dates) | per Creem's policy |
| **Expo** (push service) | your device's push token, and each notification's text: “A letter came back.”, or the first line of a post from Wren or of our reply to your note | per Expo's policy |
| **Google** | Google sign-in (name, email, profile details); the fonts every page of kindredly.io loads, which shows Google your IP address and browser | per Google's policy |
| **Spotify / YouTube** | a song link you share in a room in the app, and your IP address, when the app looks up the song's title | per their policies |
| **Email providers** | sending sign-in codes; holding mail sent to hello@kindredly.io | per provider's policy |

**About the AI.** Text is sent to Moonshot AI for two jobs only. Safety screening: in the app, the letters you write for the desk in the composer; on the website, the letters, replies and messages you type (not folded notes, photos or song links). Translation: in the app, when you tap to translate; on the website, foreign-language letters on your desk as they appear (unless you turn that off in Settings), and a kindred's messages when you tap. Only the text concerned is sent (plus the target language for a translation), and it is not attached to your name, email, account ID or IP address. Our server calls Moonshot's service in mainland China. Wren's other behaviour — how she reads your writing, what she notices about two people, the shape of your courier — is computed on your device with no model involved.

We also disclose information when the law requires it, or where necessary to protect someone's safety.

**What other members see:** your letters, and — only after five letters each way and only if you both choose — the pen name and face you enclose. Never your email, your birth date, or your fingerprint. The newest open letters are also shown, unsigned, to visitors on kindredly.io who have not made an account.

## 4 · International transfers

Your information may be stored and processed outside your country: in the regions listed above, including mainland China when Moonshot AI checks or translates text, and by Kindredly itself, which is run by a company in Beijing — for example when we read and answer a note you send Wren or a message you send through Write to us. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5 · How long we keep it

- **Your account and what you send:** until you delete your account.
- **Deleted at once with your account:** your email, pen name, birth date, pronoun, courier and first line; your fingerprint and your list of matches (other writers' lists drop you at the next daily match); your blocks; your notification tokens and records; your notes to Wren and our replies; your purchase records; folded notes you wrote, along with the other person's answers to them; and reports other people filed about you, with any suspension they caused.
- **Kept after deletion, no longer tied to an account:** letters you left on the desk (kept in our database and taken off every desk); your side of each correspondence, which stays with the other person — unsigned, unless you traded names, in which case they keep the pen name and face you gave them (a correspondence closed by a block stays closed to both); photos, screenshots and voice letters you uploaded, which stay at their addresses; messages sent through Write to us, with any reply address you left; reports you filed; product events; and any line or page you let drift out of your journal into the shared morning pool in an earlier version.
- **Backups:** database backups can hold a copy until they expire.
- **Purchases:** deleting does not cancel a subscription. Creem and RevenueCat keep their own records.

## 6 · Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- **See and export** what we hold — ask us.
- **Correct** your account details in the app.
- **Delete your account** — Settings → Delete account. This is immediate and cannot be undone; §5 says what it removes and what stays.
- **Withdraw permissions** — photo library, microphone and notifications, in iOS Settings.
- **Object or complain** — write to us, and if you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Two automatic steps can limit what you can do, with no person deciding: the safety check can hold a letter back, and when three different people report your account, it can no longer send letters or messages. If you think either was wrong, write to us. Matching orders whose letters you see — by your writing fingerprint and, gently, your birth date and pronoun; it does not decide anything else about you.

## 7 · Security

Letters are encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by database row-level security: the letter that opens a correspondence is an open letter, which anyone on the desk could read; after that, among members only the two people in it can read its replies and messages, enforced by the database itself rather than by the app. Photos and voice letters are different: each is stored at an unlisted web address, and anyone who has that address can open it without signing in. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee against every attack.

## 8 · Children

Kindredly is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

We will update this policy as the product changes, and change the date at the top. For material changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
