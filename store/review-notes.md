# App Review Notes — Kindredly 1.0

**Kindredly is a slow-correspondence app. Replies are measured in days, by
design — this is the product, not a defect.** The app says so on its own
Today screen: "Nothing's come back yet — it's too soon. Answers take hours,
sometimes days. That's the point."

## How to see the whole product in one session

1. **Sign in** — "Just looking" creates an anonymous account instantly; email
   sign-in sends a 6-digit code; Apple and Google sign-in also work.
   Onboarding requires a date of birth and blocks anyone under 18.
2. **Today tab** — a pool of letters from strangers. Swipe between them.
3. **Reply to one** — a correspondence opens. *A real answer may take days;
   you will not receive one during review, and that is expected.*
4. **Kindreds tab** — correspondences live here. Tap one to open the room.
5. **Me tab** — your writing is read into nine dimensions, on-device.

## Moderation (guideline 1.2)

- **Filter** — every open letter passes an automated safety check before it
  is delivered; all text is additionally screened for contact details.
- **Report** — the ··· menu on any letter or correspondence → REPORT THIS
  LETTER. Reports are recorded and reviewed.
- **Block** — the same menu → BLOCK THIS PERSON. Blocking is immediate and
  two-way; blocked people disappear from the pool.
- **Suspension** — accounts that break the rules are suspended server-side
  and cannot start correspondences or send messages.
- **Contact** — hello@kindredly.io, also published at kindredly.io/support.

## Account deletion (5.1.1(v))

Settings → Delete account. Immediate and permanent, no email required.
Letters already sent stay in the world unsigned, because they were written
to strangers who are still holding them; everything identifying is removed.

## In-app purchases

First Class is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly / annual) and
Cofounder is a one-time non-consumable. All three are managed through
RevenueCat and verified in sandbox.

## Age rating

Rated 18+ by override. The calculated rating was lower, but the Terms of Use
and Privacy Policy both require users to be 18 or over, and the app enforces
that at sign-up — the rating is aligned with the product, not with the
minimum the questionnaire allowed.

## Content in the pool

The letters visible at launch were written by us and posted from anonymous
accounts, so the app is not empty for its first readers. Kindredly has no
profiles, no photographs and no names before the fifth letter, so nothing
here presents itself as a person it is not.
